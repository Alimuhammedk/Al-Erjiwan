# Product lens
Score: 7/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The experience is clearly aimed at the right moment: a customer worried about choosing a colour sees it on a wall before buying.
- The plan owns the whole journey: browse, colour, order, WhatsApp, status page, FAQ. Fewer hand-offs means fewer places to break.
- Fully Arabic, mobile first and Ali-controlled admin show attention to the real user, not a template.
- The brand identity is distinctive and the design follows the logo.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Too much for a first release.** Colour picker, gallery, tinted and ready-made products, delivery zones and tracking is a lot. If any part is rough, the whole thing feels unfinished. The two-stage launch helps; hold to it.
2. **The hand-off between the site and WhatsApp.** The customer taps a button and the order text appears. If that step is clumsy, or Ali's replies are slow, the experience breaks where it matters most.
3. **Colour on screen is not colour on the wall.** The room preview promises something screens cannot guarantee. It needs honest wording and a physical-sample or tinting option.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what is the one thing this site must do brilliantly?
- What does the first five minutes look like for a customer who arrives on their phone from Instagram?
- Who answers WhatsApp, and how fast, and what do they do when the shop is closed?
- What do you cut if time runs short?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Name the one thing: my suggestion is "choose a colour with confidence and order in under two minutes".
- Make the first release excellent at browsing, ordering and the WhatsApp hand-off before any extras.
- Word the colour preview honestly, and decide what happens when a colour looks different on delivery.
