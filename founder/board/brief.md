# Board brief: Al-Erjiwan online store

Facts only, taken from the owner's spec and our interview. No opinions.

- An existing physical paint shop in Al-Kefil, Babil, Iraq, run by Ali. It sells interior and exterior paint, wood finishes, wallpaper, decor items and tools. Brand carried: Al-Jawhera Paints.
- Plan: an Arabic-only website where customers browse, pick sizes (for example 1L, 4L, 18L) and colours, and place orders. Delivery across all of Iraq with a fee that varies by governorate.
- Payment: cash on delivery only at launch. ZainCash and Qi Card may come later.
- Currency: Iraqi dinar only.
- Paint is sold two ways: some products are tinted to the customer's chosen colour, others are ready-made in fixed colours.
- A colour picker lets customers click a colour, see an illustrated room in that colour, then jump to matching products. The colour catalogue is still to be supplied by Ali.
- Orders are saved to a database and sent to the shop by WhatsApp. Customers can check order status online.
- Ali edits prices, stock, photos, banners and orders himself in a private admin area.
- Today, customers message the shop on WhatsApp about price, stock, colour choice and order status.
- Success after three months, in Ali's words as relayed: more orders and fewer WhatsApp messages answered by hand.
- Launch: as soon as possible, in two stages (shop that takes orders first, extras after).
- Unknown so far: product prices, the number of products, who the target buyer is, marketing budget, competitor landscape, delivery logistics and cost per governorate, whether Ali already has online customers.
