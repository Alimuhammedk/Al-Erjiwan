# Al-Erjiwan (أصباغ الأرجوان) online store

- What it is: an online store for an existing paint and decor shop in Al-Kefil, Babil, Iraq.
- Who it is for: customers across Iraq buying paint and decor, mostly on mobile phones. Not yet narrowed to a specific buyer type (homeowners, painters or contractors); the owner has not said.
- What it sells, at what price: interior and exterior paint, wood finishes, wallpaper, decor items and tools, under the brand Al-Jawhera Paints. Prices are not yet set; Ali supplies them later.
- Where and how: the website is in Arabic. Customers order online and pay cash on delivery, with a delivery fee that varies by governorate. Orders are saved and sent to the store by WhatsApp. Customers can pick a colour on a colour picker with a room preview.
- Budget and constraints: speed matters ("as soon as possible"). Stack is Next.js and Supabase on Vercel. The owner, Ali, must own all accounts and be able to run everything himself. Success after three months: more orders and fewer WhatsApp messages answered by hand. Budget is not stated.
