# Board summary: Al-Erjiwan online store

## The vote
3 FUND IF, 0 fund, 0 pass. Average score 6.0 / 10 (Offers 6, Monopoly 5, Product 7).

## Risks more than one member raised
1. **No clear reason to buy from Al-Erjiwan beyond convenience** (Offers, Monopoly). A site alone is easy to copy and invites a price fight.
2. **Trust in delivery and colour** (Offers, Product). Colour on a screen may not match the wall, and delivery across Iraq may arrive late or damaged. There is no guarantee yet.
3. **Reaching customers is unplanned** (Monopoly, Offers). The plan covers the site, not how the first 100 customers arrive.
4. **Too much for a first release** (Product, Monopoly's "all Iraq at once"). Both point to starting smaller.

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)
- [ ] Decide the target buyer: homeowners, painters or contractors. (Offers) Consumer panel
- [ ] A guarantee for damaged or wrong-colour orders that the margin can afford. (Offers) CFO, offer
- [ ] A litres calculator so customers do not guess quantities. (Offers) Offer
- [ ] One line on why a customer in another governorate orders from Kefil. (Offers, Monopoly) Competitors, marketing
- [ ] Pick a first small market (Babil and neighbours) and prove it before promoting nationwide. (Monopoly) Launch
- [ ] Establish what is exclusive: brand, stock, tinting or local expertise. (Monopoly) Competitors
- [ ] Write down how the first 100 customers are reached and what each costs. (Monopoly) Marketing, CFO
- [ ] Name the one thing the site does brilliantly. Suggested: choose a colour with confidence and order in under two minutes. (Product)
- [ ] Hold the two-stage launch; make browse, order and the WhatsApp hand-off excellent first. (Product)
- [ ] Honest wording on colour preview, and a plan for when a colour looks different. (Product)
- [ ] Who answers WhatsApp, how fast, and when the shop is closed. (Product) Ops

## Open questions (numbers nobody has yet)
Prices and margins, delivery cost per governorate, how many online customers Ali already has, who sells paint online in Iraq today, and what Al-Jawhera exclusivity really means. These go to `/founder-cfo`, `/founder-competitors` and `/founder-consumer`.

## The strongest version of this business
A local paint shop that wins its nearest governorates first by removing the fear of choosing wrong: a colour picker, a litres calculator, honest colour wording and a damaged or wrong-colour guarantee, all ordered in two minutes from a phone. Online sales grow outward from Babil as delivery proves reliable. That is narrower than "all Iraq", and the board thinks it is stronger.

## Where the members disagreed
Monopoly scored lowest (5) because nothing stops a copycat, while Product scored highest (7) because the experience design is thoughtful. They read the same plan differently: one asks "can anyone copy it", the other asks "is it good". The answer to the first depends on the brand exclusivity Ali has not confirmed.
